# DROP_SWAP, 18 November 2026 (America/New_York)

Midnight research prototype, off by default. `rules.json` and every test threshold are unchanged. This write-up finishes the 17 November 11:52 PM ET run, which scored the cohort and then was cut off before clips and a report.

## Why this idea

Carry-over from [docs/GRAPHCUT_2026-11-13.md](GRAPHCUT_2026-11-13.md): "later bass handoff when the incoming has no bass in the window." BASS_COVER, graph-cut fader-follow bass, and the mid-track window planner were already tried. What had not been tried is re-cueing the *same* outro-over-intro window so the incoming's first drop lands on the bass swap — the classic drum-and-bass move of swapping the bass in one decisive move, usually right on the drop (the Vande Veire & De Bie 2018 cue family, already reported on 10 November, now actually coded).

STRUCT's default outro-over-intro window cues the incoming at drop1 − X, so its drop lands one bar *after* the blend ends. With `FADE=long` the bass swap sits at the exit start (about bar 6–7 of a 16-bar window). When the incoming intro has no bass, the low end is handed to a bass-less intro and the summed lows fall into a hole until the drop (Final Hours → Listen 44.5 dB on the earlier graph-cut pair; Prime Expansion → Demons 22.5 dB).

the listener also prefers mid-track or drop cues over a safe outro-into-intro blend. This flag only tries the first drop, not a later one.

## Prototype: `DROP_SWAP=1` (`src2/drop_swap.py`)

Off by default. Per-pair gate, not a blanket rule:

- Fire only when every bar of the default STRUCT window's incoming overlap is at least 6 dB under the incoming's own body (the existing SWAP_THIN / BASS_COVER thin line; nothing loosened).
- Nominate one extra window with the same outgoing end and the incoming cued at drop1 − d, where d is the 4-bar phrase boundary at or after the start of the bass handoff, so incoming bass is at body when outgoing bass leaves.
- The original window stays in the candidate set. Both carry the STRUCT bonus; the engine's own cost chooses.
- Skip the new cue if it would start before bar 0, run off the end of the track, equal the default cue, or if the incoming is not actually at body (within 6 dB) for at least half of the post-drop bars inside the window (a mis-detected drop1).

X1, GAP_DB, F1/N1 and the swap-position rule are untouched. 8 unit tests in `src/tests/test_drop_swap.py`. The like-for-like cohort is `protrans/eval_drop_swap.sh` (variants `d1118_base` and `ds1118_dropswap`).

Computed overnight; scoring finished 12:41 AM ET on 18 November. Baseline matches the current floor: train **32.40**, held-out **34.07**, scorer n_fail **0**.

Held-out fell 2.83 points and train fell 2.69. The change does not beat the held-out floor of 34.07, and it regresses train 32.40, so it is **not kept**. The bass hole the flag was built to fix did get shallower (median dip 23.6 → 12.5 dB). The scorer still preferred the default windows.

The gate fired on **17/33** pairs (16 worse, 1 slightly better). The one gain was Sabre "These Simple Things" → Hybrid Minds "Pretend (ft. Rocky Nti)" (`hybrid_minds_ukf__12`), 12.03 → 12.51. The worst held-out loss was Pola & Bryson feat. Hugh Hardie "Vetur" → Pola & Bryson & Ko-Jo "Closer to Home" (`pola_bryson_puzzlewood__00`): **59.25 → 34.92**, but the dip improved **45.4 → 11.1 dB**. Other large losses: Secret Place → These Simple Things (`hybrid_minds_ukf__11`) 34.15 → 20.16; Final Hours → Listen (`hybrid_minds_ukf__08`) 42.98 → 32.21; Cinnamon → Together (`hybrid_minds_ukf__25`) 26.37 → 18.42.

Must-pass composition changed but the 29/33 count did not: drop-swap cleared the L1 on Listen → The Optimist (`hybrid_minds_ukf__09`) and added an N1 half-beat fail on Find My Way → The Feeling (`joe_syntax_hospital__00`, lag −11.61 ms vs a 10 ms line). The other three N1 fails were already on the default and did not move.

## This is not the Hybrid Minds Cinnamon → Together cue

The 15 November SNAP_IN write-up found that Hybrid Minds cued Logistics "Together" at its **third** drop (263.6 s, bar 191) over Cinnamon's breakdown, not the intro. `DROP_SWAP` only re-cues the **first** drop onto the bass swap, and only when the default intro overlap is all thin. On this pair the flag did fire and the scorer still dropped (26.37 → 18.42). A first-drop re-cue cannot reproduce a later-drop pro cue. The next step is a per-pair picker over intro / drop1 / later drops, not a global on-switch.
