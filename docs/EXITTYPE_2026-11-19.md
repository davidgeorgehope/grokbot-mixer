# Two-stage exit-type picker, 2026-11-19 (day run, about 6:25 to 7:10 AM ET)

**Outcome: not kept, not promoted. `rules.json` and defaults are unchanged.** No fresh mix was spent, because the dev CV showed no clear win over picker_1119b and the changed picks hard-fail more often than the default.

**What we learned:**
- The exit type (mid-track drop swap vs riding to the outro) is a **DJ habit**. Repeating the DJ's last mode is right 79.8% of the time.
- A calibrated online prior gets AUC 0.83. But within a DJ there is no signal: within-DJ AUC is 0.49 even with style, and 0.505 without it.
- **Style-free models have no signal.** Five tries (pair content, mix-level audio proxies, teacher/student, mix-level commit, structure-only) all land at AUC 0.30–0.54, with no end-to-end win.
- **the listener's call at 7:04 AM ET:** both modes sound fine, so stop predicting style. Treat the exit type as a structure-weighted coin flip, judge each option on its own placement and the hard checks, and focus on audible faults. The locked Liquicity/Hospital holdout was **not** opened. The John B and OffbeatDooter fresh mixes stay spent.

## Design
- **Labels:** "mid" means the pro's exit entry is at least 32 bars before the outgoing track's music end; otherwise it's "late". Dev has 148 pairs from 19 mixes and 9 DJs; 92 are mid and 56 late.
- **Stage 1** is a calibrated P(mid) classifier. The model family (LightGBM, L2 logistic, constant base rate, and for the style version an online beta-binomial mode prior or a style-only logistic) and the calibration (none, Platt or isotonic) are chosen by inner leave-one-DJ-out log loss. Calibrators are cross-fitted across inner DJs.
- **Features:**
  - section structure (drop-1 end, outro length, breakdown, intro length of the incoming track);
  - per-bar energy and bass curves (from `src2/st/<id>.json`);
  - roughness of the mid window vs the late window (`src2/consonance.py`);
  - track lengths, BPM, Camelot key distance (essentia `edma`);
  - **style only:** online history from the same mix, index < t only;
  - **r4 only:** mix-level audio and structure proxies (playlist means of drop-1 length and bass, outro length, breakdown depth, incoming intro length). These use no pro decisions, so they would also work for the listener's mixes.
- **Stage 2:** the expected-cost rule chooses mid iff p·C[m,m] + (1−p)·C[m,l] < p·C[l,m] + (1−p)·C[l,l]. C is the inner-OOF mean exit error of the within-type pick, typically [[22–34, 54–73], [76–80, 8–14]]. picker_1119b's exit ranker then places the bar inside the chosen type. Its cue ranker and X classifier are unchanged.
- **Variants:** `cost`; `thr` (threshold tuned on inner OOF); `abst` (abstain to the default inside a band around the break-even p, tuned on inner OOF); `oracle` (true type, a ceiling, not a policy); `knob_mid` and `knob_late` (fixed style, diagnostic); `knob_bestpermix` and `knob_worstpermix` (fixed style chosen per mix with hindsight: diagnostic upper and lower bounds, not a user option).
- **Eval:** leave-one-DJ-out on dev, nested for all tuning. Mix-grouped bootstrap 95% CIs (4000 draws). Error is the exit+cue bar error vs the pro (mean of the two), lower is better.

## Iterations and why each won or missed
1. **r1: bug.** Every fold picked "constant + Platt" and the pooled AUC was 0.12. Leave-one-DJ-out base rates are anti-correlated with the held-out DJ's own rate, so the Platt step learned a negative slope. Fix: the constant is never calibrated, and "no calibration" is now an option.
2. **r2:** the style classifier reached AUC 0.66, but its log loss (0.68) was worse than the base rate (0.663). End-to-end it was a tie with picker_1119b (Δ −0.96 [−4.13, +3.66]).
3. **r3: an online beta-binomial mode prior** (a running "how often has this DJ exited mid so far" with a shrinkage prior). Nested CV chose it in every fold (a = 0.5–1, no decay):
   - **calibrated:** AUC 0.83, log loss 0.488 vs 0.663 base, Brier 0.156, ECE 0.06, choice accuracy 80.4%;
   - **end-to-end `two_style_cost`:** 24.17 vs default 37.67, **Δ −13.51 [−20.17, −3.35]**;
   - **vs picker_1119b style_gated: Δ −2.24 [−4.99, +1.64]**, so no clear difference;
   - exit hit@16 0.622 vs 0.297 default (Δ +0.32 [+0.06, +0.52]); cue hit@16 0.581 vs 0.601.
4. **No-style classifier:** AUC 0.30 (r2/r3), worse than chance. It was **not** a label or sign flip: the style model uses the same labels and reaches 0.83. It is a leave-one-DJ-out artifact. Within each DJ the AUC is 0.505 (pair-weighted), but across DJs the mean predicted p is anti-correlated with the DJ's real mid rate (r = −0.61). MrMoMMusic (90% mid) got mean p 0.50; Alpha Rhythm (5% mid) and KushSessions (12%) got about 0.66. Flipping it would not generalize. Track content doesn't predict the exit type within a DJ. Between DJs it only carries confounds (65% of MrMoMMusic pairs lack local audio, see the next section).
   - **r4 with mix-level audio proxies:** the no-style AUC rises to 0.507 (the artifact is gone), but there is still no signal. End-to-end `two_nostyle_cost` is +0.58 [−7.10, +8.18] vs default, and +11.85 [+5.03, +16.36] vs picker_1119b (worse).
   - The style model's within-DJ AUC is also only 0.49 (DJ-level r = 0.95). **So all of the exit-type signal is the DJ's per-mix habit. Nothing tells which pair within a mix gets the other mode.**
5. **Threshold (`thr`) and abstention (`abst`)** didn't help: Δ vs picker_1119b −1.02 [−3.82, +2.63] and −0.57 [−3.59, +3.32].
6. **Fixed-style bounds (diagnostic only, not a user-facing option; the listener doesn't want to pick a style):**
   - always mid: 33.36, Δ vs default −4.32 [−14.95, +11.18];
   - always late: 37.46, −0.21 [−2.35, +1.46];
   - **best fixed style per mix (upper bound): 23.27, Δ vs default −14.40 [−19.30, −7.38], vs picker_1119b −3.14 [−6.75, −0.33]** (the only arm with a CI below picker_1119b);
   - worst per mix (lower bound): 47.55, +9.88 [+4.84, +17.82].

   So getting the mode right per mix is worth about 14 bars of pro-match. Under the listener's 7:04 AM ET call, that pro-match gap no longer matters.
6b. **r5, style-free, after the listener's 7:02 AM ET "no picking" note:**
   - **teacher/student:** a LightGBM student learns from content to predict the online style teacher's soft labels;
   - **mix-level commit:** one mode per generated mix from the mean style-free P(mid);
   - **results:** no-style AUC 0.538, log loss 0.725 vs 0.663 base. `two_nostyle_cost` −2.07 [−10.15, +4.37] vs default and +9.20 [+0.12, +14.70] vs picker_1119b. `commit_nostyle` −1.32 [−10.70, +5.35]. No signal.
   - An extended-corpus run (`exittype-cv-1119-r5ext`: 304 accepted rows, holdout DJs and spent fresh mixes still excluded) was still running at wrap-up. Its result goes in `exittype_1119_cv_r5ext.json` and is not used here.
7. **Ceiling:** with the true type, `oracle_style` reaches 18.98 (Δ vs picker_1119b −7.43 [−10.01, −3.99]). The within-type ranker is good; the mode decision is the bottleneck.

## Proposed policy: `EXITTYPE=structcoin` (flag only, off; not in rules.json)
- **Type choice:** a seeded coin flip on the pair id, weighted by what the outgoing track's structure supports:
  - P(mid) = 0 if there's no drop 1 or fewer than 48 bars follow its end;
  - otherwise 0.5, +0.15 if a breakdown follows drop 1, −0.15 if the outro is at least 32 bars.
- **Bar placement:** the style-free picker_1119b exit ranker places the bar inside the chosen type. Cue and X are unchanged.
- **Hard checks** only veto.
- **Dev LODO results** (`exittype_1119_coin.py` → `exittype_1119_coin.json`). Hard checks cover only windows already rendered (pick1119, et1119c, et1119k), vs the rendered default on the same pairs:

| arm | chose mid | HC checked | HC_A pick / default (same pairs) | HC_B pick / default | new-fail checks fired | exit off section (>4 bars) | exit off 16-bar phrase | pro bar error (ref.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| default | 0% | 91 | 13 / 13 | 11 / 11 | – | 66.9% | 8.1% | 37.67 |
| **structcoin** | 56.8% | 58 | **14 (24.1%) / 9 (15.5%)** | 10 / 7 | pump 3, hole 2, clash v1 2, clash v2 1 | 43.2% | 23.6% | 36.16 (Δ −1.52 [−8.47, +8.41]) |
| fixed mid | 100% | 55 | 13 (23.6%) / 8 (14.5%) | 11 / 6 | pump 5, hole 2, clash v1 2 | 29.1% | 23.0% | 33.36 |
| fixed late | 0% | 61 | 12 (19.7%) / 10 (16.4%) | 8 / 7 | clash v1 2, hole 1 | 69.6% | 27.7% | 37.46 |
| picker_1119b sg | 50% | 81 | 15 (18.5%) / 12 (14.8%) | 14 / 10 | pump 7 | 43.2% | 20.9% | 26.40 |

- **Readout:** the coin flip itself is not the problem. Learned placement inside a mode adds faults:
  - **Mid windows** blend inside full-energy drops, so **pumping** is the main new fault.
  - **Late windows** pushed to the last bar run past the outro, causing **bass holes** (e.g. `sound_territory_p5JM5r2J8r0__15`, v2 hole score 148).
  - The ranker's exits land **off the 16-bar phrase** on 21–28% of pairs, vs 8% for the default.
- **Not promotable as is:** HC_A 24% vs 15.5% for the default.
- **Mid-vocal check:** not possible on dev. Corpus tracks' `st/*.json` have no vocal curve (no demucs stems), so vocal overlap is unmeasured.
