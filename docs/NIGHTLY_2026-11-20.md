## Result 2: hard-check detectors (priority a), cached arrays only, no renders
Method (unchanged rule, nothing hand-loosened): every threshold is the 95th percentile of the same statistic on the 33 real pro blends (so about 5% of real pro blends would be flagged; "pro FA" is the leave-one-pro-out false-alarm rate). Design choices are made on the dev half of the injected-fault set; the held-out third (30 renders × each fault) is reported afterwards. Injected faults: a 4- or 8-bar bass hole, one deck shifted 60 ms or 110 ms (beat clash), and a level pump.

Held-out detection (share of injected faults caught) and clean false alarms:

| detector | clean renders flagged | 4-bar hole | 8-bar hole | 60 ms clash | 110 ms clash | pro FA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bass hole v2 (official, T0=30) | 0% | 30% | 90% | – | – | ~5% |
| bass hole v3 (T0=38, from 11-19, `hole_t0_1119.json`) | 6.7% | **80%** | **97%** | – | – | 6.1% |
| clash v2 (official, median lag) | 3.3% | – | – | 17% | 57% | ~9% |
| **clash "frac-off" (new tonight, `clash_frac_1120.py`)** | 3.3% | – | – | **67%** | **73%** | 6.1% |

- Why clash v2 misses: the pro p95 of the median lag is 71–106 ms (pro blends are measured on the finished mix recording, so their lag estimate is noisy), which sits above a 60 ms clash. The new statistic asks "what share of the confidently measured overlap bars are more than 40 ms apart" (bars with deck-to-deck correlation ≥ 0.2, at least 5 such bars, else it abstains). That share is robust to a few noisy pro bars. Chosen on dev: (c 0.2, 5 bars, 40 ms), threshold 0.83.
- Still below the 80% recall bar for clashes, so it is **not** adopted as a gate; hole v3 meets 80% at 4 bars and stays the recommended bass-hole check (pending a decision to switch the official set).
- On real renders the new clash check rarely agrees with the old v1 clash flag (default arm 103 renders: v1 7, frac 3, both 1), consistent with v1 being the known false-alarmer.
- Per-check counts on rendered arms (default / pick1119): v1 clash 7/6, v2 clash 3/4, frac 3/3, hole v2 1/4, hole v3 4/5, pump 9/17. Table: `protrans/replica/detector_table_1120.json`.
