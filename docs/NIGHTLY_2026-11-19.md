## Diagnosis: where the style-aware model helps and where it misses (dev, per pair)
- The gain is all from style. `nostyle_gated` is worse than default (+4.93) and `nostyle_plain` is flat. Online style only exists when recreating a pro mix, so the model **isn't deployable for the listener's own mixes** without a real style source.
- **Helps:** when the pro exits mid-track, at least 32 bars before the outgoing track ends (92 pairs), the mean Δ is **-21.5 bars**. The model jumps to the drop-1 end (around bar 97 to 113) where the default plays to the outro. Typical wins are -48 to -56 bars: MrMoMMusic, TheHeshBesh, UKF, SuicideSheeep `__15`.
  - By DJ: MrMoMMusic -24.9 (32 better / 6 worse), TheHeshBesh -25.1, UKF -13.9, Fryett -13.3, John B -7.1, Sound Territory -5.6.
- **Misses:** when the pro exits late, in the outro (56 pairs), the mean Δ is **+6.5**, and individual misses cost +32 to +48 bars. Examples: `alpha_rhythm_*__01/__03/__05/__06/__07`, `mrmommusic_nodB5gqt_mU__29/__41/__44/__66`, `suicidesheeep_R8MWKsheHxk__09`. The style history said "mid-track", so the model jumped about 96 bars early.
  - Alpha Rhythm is the one DJ that's net worse (+12.6; 2 better / 10 worse), because his mixes alternate between the two exit modes.
  - First transitions of a mix have no history, and they average +6.4.
- **Why:** the exit error is bimodal (about 0 or about 96 bars). The ranker's margin gate works on a ranking score, not on the cost of picking the wrong mode, so one wrong mode guess costs more than several right ones save.

## Clash detector: not adopted
Thresholds are always the pro p95 (leave-one-pro-out false alarms). Design was picked on dev injected cases, and the held-out split is md5 % 3.

| statistic | chosen design | pro FA | held-out clean flagged | held-out 60 ms recall | held-out 110 ms recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| off-grid onset share (`clash_grid_1119.py`) | tol 18 ms, p25, thr 0.1134 | 6.1% | 16.7% | **13.3%** | **20.0%** |
| flam autocorrelation (`clash_flam_1119.py`) | 35 to 140 ms, median, thr 0.3735 | 9.1% | 3.3% | 0% | 3.3% |

Both are far below the 80% recall bar. For the grid statistic, clean renders are flagged as often as real 60 ms clashes, so it isn't separating them at all. Thresholds were not hand-loosened. The hard-check set stays HC_A (v2 hole, v1 clash, half-beat, pump).

Side result, not adopted tonight: the bass-hole T0=38 variant (`hole_t0_1119.json`, pro p95 threshold 36) catches 4-bar holes at 80% held-out (v2: 30%) and 8-bar holes at 97%, with 6.7% clean flags and 6.1% pro false alarms. It's worth a full HC_C re-count next night.

## Next idea to try
Replace the margin gate with a **two-stage exit-mode decision**:
1. A calibrated classifier for "mid-track exit vs outro exit", using track-content features plus a style prior.
2. An **expected-cost (Bayes) choice** that jumps mid-track only when P(mid) × gain is greater than P(outro) × loss, with conformal abstention back to the default.

Then the ranker only places the bar within the chosen mode. The style prior must come from a deployable source (the listener's per-mix setting, or the mix's own earlier *planned* transitions), and the no-style version must win dev on its own. Also:
