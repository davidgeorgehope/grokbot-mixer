# BASS_WAIT (2026-11-27): hold the outgoing lows until the incoming sub-bass is present

## Why n1127 skipped ridegate_lock_pump_ee and profade_ex35

Those two arms were on the n1127 card (`candidates_n1127` / `n1127_run.py`). The routine that actually ran overnight launched `jobs/render-n1127` -> `replica/run_render_1127.sh`, which hardcoded the two drum-clash landing-fade arms (`dclash_land6`, `dclash_land7`) plus hard checks on the already-rendered default. It never read the card.

Fix for n1128: `replica/run_nightly_card.sh` (and `run_render_1128.sh`, a thin wrapper) always launches `n1128_run.py`, which iterates `nightly_card.card_for("n1128")`. Arm names do not live in the shell script. The n1128 card is the three `epg_*` arms, the two skipped shadows, and `bass_wait` (six-arm cap). Tests in `protrans/tests/test_nightly_n1128_card.py` lock that in.

## What BASS_WAIT does

Off by default (`BASS_WAIT=1` to turn on). After the usual bass swap is planned, the outgoing low-lane cut is delayed until the incoming track's lows sit within `BASS_WAIT_DB` of its drop-body level for two bars. The crossfader and the window stay put. If incoming bass never arrives in the window, the outgoing lows stay up and ride the fader out. PIN swap_bars still wins. `apply_rules` passes `BASS_WAIT` / `BASS_WAIT_DB`. Nothing is written to `rules.json` unless this later ships. Rollback in that case: `BASS_WAIT_OFF=1`.

`BASS_WAIT_DB = 6.2 dB`: that is the pro p95 of (incoming drop-body minus incoming 90 Hz at the pro's bass-swap bar) on official swaps already in or within 8 bars of drop 1 (n=8). The unconditional p95 over all 26 measurable official pairs is 49.75 dB, because several swaps land in the incoming intro or build, where there is no sub-bass on purpose. That figure is not a presence threshold. Calib: `replica/nightly_1128_work/bass_wait_calib.json`.

## The hole sensor

`replica/lows_hole.py` is the 90 Hz width-aware V-dip from the 11-27 hchole90 work. Threshold = pro p95 25.21 dB on the 33 official pro clips, so flags on clean pro transitions stay at the pro rate (2 of 33). A hole that comes back is scored; a lows drop that never returns is not. The official bass-hole check still almost never sees these gaps.

## Conclusion

**Not promoted.** The idea is pointed the right way (one real hole closed, one pump fail gone, mean v1/v2 up) but it is not a ship-it win: the CIs include 0, two pairs lose clearly, four sensor holes remain, 9 of 27 dev pairs did not render, and the fresh mix could not be scored. BASS_WAIT stays off and is shadowed on the n1128 card. The ridegate+LOCK+PUMP default is unchanged.

What's next: look at the two bass-stem losses before any defaulting; make the wait fire on the unchanged deep holes (or combine with a later bass-swap that waits for incoming sub-bass when the arrival is after the window); score the fresh mix once its audio is on disk; let n1128 actually render ee, profade, and bass_wait from the card.
