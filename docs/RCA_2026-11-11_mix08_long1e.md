## 1. Bass gap, trans 18, bar 1901 (2 bars)

Hybrid Minds ft. Catching Cairo – Supernova → Hybrid Minds ft. Alexa Harley – Solitude. Window 1872–1904 (43:02–43:46 ET mix time). Incoming starts at its own bar 16.

**What the test does.** `src2/bassgap.py`: in the overlap, a mixdown low (<150 Hz) bar that sits more than 9.84 dB under the median of its ±4 neighbours, for more than 1 bar, fails unless *both* source tracks themselves dip ≥ GAP_DB/2 there.

**Like-for-like vs v2.** The hole is in Solitude. Source lows (pre-fader) in the second half of the window fall 8–32 dB under that track’s first-half median (source bars 40–47: lows −36, −44, −40, −50, −51, −55, −60, −40). Supernova’s own lows stay high (~−20 dB) through the end of the window, so the “both sources dip” exemption does not apply.

Mixdown depths at the official fail:

| bar | long1e mix / neighbour / depth | v2 mix / neighbour / depth | flag long / v2 |
|-----|----------------------------------|----------------------------|----------------|
| 1900 | −41.5 / −35.4 / 6.1 | −40.3 / −35.2 / 5.1 | no / no |
| 1901 | −45.7 / −35.4 / **10.2** | −42.7 / −35.2 / 7.4 | **yes** / no |
| 1902 | −50.5 / −33.6 / **16.9** | −46.3 / −33.6 / 12.7 | **yes** / **yes** |
| 1903 | −36.4 / −28.3 / 8.1 | −36.3 / −27.8 / 8.5 | no / no |
| 1904 | −15.6 (Solitude drop) | −15.6 | no / no |

v2 also has a 12.7 dB hole at 1902, but only one bar, so the official test allows it (L≤1). long1e is 3–4 dB deeper at 1901–1902 because the outgoing is already gone (x=1 at bar 1900). That tips 1901 over 9.84 dB and makes a 2-bar run. **New as an official failure under FADE=long.** The musical hole exists in v2; the extra bar and the extra depth are new. long1 (solo=16, gone at 1888) failed the same way; long1c (exit delayed to the window end) passed this test and then failed V1.

**Code path.** `_ctx` in `mix_simple.py`: `struct_solo` (stays 16 — Supernova has no 4 dB body drop in the window) → `cap_solo_incoming` (sees Solitude’s second-half low crash, last thin bar +30, **caps solo to 0**, exit would end at 1904 and cover the hole, which is what long1c did) → `solo_before_incoming_vocal` (incoming voc at window bars +23, +25, +26, +27 is above voc-body−12; **raises solo to 4** so outgoing gain is already under 0.3 there). Exit then ends at 1900, one bar before the hole. `solo_before` slides a fixed 8-bar exit earlier; it does not lengthen the exit, so it cannot be quiet at the vocal *and* still be present at 1901–1902.

**Ranked causes.**

1. `solo_before_incoming_vocal` undoes `cap_solo_incoming`. This is the root cause. The two helpers fight: one stays to cover a thin incoming stretch, the other leaves early to avoid a vocal clash. A fixed-length exit cannot do both.
2. Solitude’s pre-drop lows (source bars 40–47). Real in the source, also visible in v2. Not an engine hole, but it becomes a 2-bar official gap once the outgoing is gone.
3. The “both sources dip” exemption cannot fire, because Supernova still has bass in the source; we just already faded it out.
4. Beat alignment is not the cause (half-beat ok, dphase 0 ms on the overlap).

**Fix (general, no threshold change).** `solo_before_incoming_vocal` must not end the exit before the last thin incoming bar (`cap_solo` is a ceiling on how early we leave). If a late vocal would still clash at that exit end, lengthen the exit (start earlier, keep the end) so outgoing gain is under 0.3 at the vocal while residual bass still covers the thin bars, staying within the 3 dB/bar fader cap. On this window: exit end stays at 1904 (solo=0), a longer exit can be under 0.3 at bar 1895 and still present through 1902 at about 2 dB/bar.

## 2. L1 at 2:45 (trans 0) and 16:00 (trans 6)

L1 in `src/guardrails.py` is two clauses. Only the first fired: sub-sum > louder deck + 3 dB, and the sum is above outgoing-body−25 dB. The second clause (“both decks carry full bass”) did not fire: these subs sit around −39 dB, more than 12 dB under the programme.

**2:45, trans 0** (Better Now → Forest Fires), bar 120, window 112–144. long bass swap [117.5, 122.5] (4-bar + BASS_LEAD 0.5+0.5), centre 120 = exit start, x=0.5. At bar 120 both rendered subs are almost equal (−38.8 and −38.6), sum −35.5, **+3.1 dB** over the louder deck. v2’s 2-bar swap is later, [122, 124]. At bar 120 v2 outgoing is still much louder than incoming (A −17.8, B −34.9, 17 dB apart), so the L1 sub clause never even runs. At v2’s own mid-swap, incoming sub is still ~−59 dB and over=0. **New to FADE=long.**

**16:00, trans 6** (Me & You → Blur), bar 696, 16-bar window 688–704, long swap [693.5, 698.5] again centred on the exit start (bar 696, x=0.5). long: subs −38.8 and −39.5, sum −35.3, **+3.5 dB**. v2 swap is [693, 695]; at bar 696 v2 already has outgoing sub down to −47.4 vs incoming −40.1, sum −38.8, over only +1.3 (under the line). Same musical moment, **new as a failure** because the long 4-bar mid-cross lands on two equal quiet subs.

**Code path.** `fade_long.swap_pos` puts the bass-swap *centre* at `exit_start` (x=xp2=0.5, legal for X1). `_ctx` forces `SW=4` under FADE=long (a 2-bar handoff stacked on this exit took 6.4 dB off Ember in long1, over the 6 dB/bar rendered-tail cap). `mix_simple.lead()` for a non-phrase-snapped swap (and a long swap is not phrase-snapped, because `swap_at` returns `POS*X`) is `(BASS_LEAD/2, BASS_LEAD/2)` = 0.5+0.5. So outgoing bass begins falling 2.5 bars *before* the exit, while both faders still sit at −3 dB on the plateau. The mid-cross is exactly the equal-sub moment. That is slightly against the stated LONG_SWAP=exit intent (Joe kept the outgoing lows full until the cut, then lows and level left together).

A 4-bar swap *starting* at the exit (centre at exit+2) would put the centre at x=0.747, which fails X1 (centre must be ≤0.65). A 2-bar swap starting at the exit (centre at exit+1, x=0.6498, legal) is the long1 Ember shape that failed the abrupt-tail test. Moving a 4-bar centre one bar into the exit still includes bar 120 in the window, so L1 would still fire there.

**Ranked causes.**

1. Low-lane timing: a 4-bar swap *centred* on the exit start, with symmetric 0.5-bar lead, mid-crosses on the plateau while both faders are at −3 dB. Root cause.
2. Two sources that happen to have similar quiet sub at that bar (Forest Fires intro, Blur intro). Other 4-bar long swaps in this mix (e.g. trans 1, trans 9) are unequal at the mid-cross and do not trip L1 (over 0.0–2.0 dB).
3. Not a “both carry full bass” problem, and not a half-beat problem.
4. Not a threshold problem: two equal signals sum ~+3 dB by arithmetic. The 3 dB line is doing what it says; the handoff is what should move.

**Fix (general, no threshold change).** Under FADE=long, use the same outgoing-leads timing already used on phrase-snapped swaps: `lead = (BASS_LEAD, 0)` so outgoing lows start leaving one bar earlier and incoming mid-crosses at the exit start. Width stays 4 bars, centre stays at the exit start (x=0.5, X1 ok), so the Ember abrupt-tail reason for SW=4 is unchanged. The mid-cross is no longer two equal plateau faders. Do not loosen L1_db and do not go back to a 2-bar swap on this exit.

## 3. N1 at 13:42, trans 5 (VdyXzg3nxfU, −11 ms)

goddard. – Green Light → Friction – Me & You. Window 592–624 (13:36–14:21). Incoming starts at its own bar 0. Official N1 is the first bar where the rendered incoming exceeds outgoing-body−24 dB, then `deckpair.beat_phase` on a 2-bar window; fail if abs(phase) > 10 ms.

**Like-for-like vs v2.** Both have the same outgoing body (refA −15, line at −39 dB).

| bar | long B (dB) | v2 B (dB) | first-audible? |
|-----|-------------|-----------|----------------|
| 595 | −41.1 | −45.5 | neither |
| 596 | **−36.2** | −43.7 | **long enters here (13:42)** |
| 597 | −35.9 | −42.3 | |
| 598 | −35.3 | −40.9 | |
| 599 | −32.7 | **−37.7** | **v2 enters here (13:46)** |

long’s rise, even with a 2-bar vocal hold, reaches x=0.35 by window bar +4. v2’s tail rise is still about −43 dB at that bar and only crosses the line three bars later. Official N1 on long is −11 ms at bar 596 (1 ms over the line). v2 is measured at bar 599 and passed. Friction – Me & You has **no** per-bar nudge in `nudge08_r9c.json`. **New as an official failure**, because the long rise moves “first audible” onto an earlier intro bar. The same source audio at bar 596 is in v2; v2 just is not yet audible there, so N1 does not look at it.

Half-beat does not fail (only two drum-on-drum bars; lag 87 ms on a sparse intro is not the gate). Per-bar A−B offsets around the entry are large and unstable for the same reason; they are not the N1 measurement.

**Code path.** `guardrails.py` N1 entry branch (not the level-step branch). First audible uses rendered incoming vs `refA-24`. `vocal_hold` delayed the rise by 2 bars on this pair; the long rise is still faster than tail, so the entry is earlier than v2, not later. `swap_at` / low lanes are not involved.

**Ranked causes.**

1. FADE=long’s early rise moves the official first-audible bar from 599 to 596 (incoming source bar 4), which is 11 ms off the 10 ms grid. Root cause of the *new fail*.
2. That intro bar has no warp nudge. The 11 ms itself is a source-grid (or first-bar onset) error, the usual N1 case. Root cause of the *milliseconds*.
3. Not a half-beat, and not a reason to loosen N1_ms=10 (pro-set, 1 ms is still over the line).

**Fix (no threshold change).** Do not delay the rise just to hide the bar. The standing N1 repair is a per-bar warp nudge (`src/autofix.py`, units are milliseconds in `nudge*.json`). Write that onto a **copy** of `nudge08_r9c.json` used only by the long test (do not edit the shipped v2 nudge file in place). Then one rebuild with the bass-gap and L1 code fixes above.
