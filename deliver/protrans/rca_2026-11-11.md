## Root causes, ranked
1. **Decks are half a beat apart.** Locating each stretched source inside Joe's mix (onset xcorr r=0.94 / 0.97, rate 1.0000)
   shows he locks the two grids 0.47 beat apart. Ours puts the grids together, so our A sits -0.458 beat (-158 ms) off Joe's lock for the whole overlap.
   A's kick and snare hits land on grid beats 1.42 / 3.42 instead of 0.95 / 2.95. Nu:Tone's grid locked onto the off-beat.
   prep.py:16-24 fits phase with a broadband-onset comb, and prep.py:29-36 / fixgrid.py only choose among 4 whole-beat downbeats, so nothing ever tests a half-beat shift.
   Per-bar fine offset (8th grid): -12..-17 ms (median -15). Drift is only about 3 ms over 50 bars, so tempo is stable and there's no wobble the grid misses.
   It's audible wherever both decks are within 12 dB: bars 221-231, clip 21-35 s. Worst at bars 226-230, clip 28-34 s, where the decks are within 6 dB of each other.
   None of the guards can see it. The rxc drum-fight check (mix_simple.py:67-74) only searches +-BEAT/8 (43 ms) and got R=0.423, a pass. flam_ms (blend_bench.py:120-127) searches +-40 ms. The scorer's beat_off was 17 ms.
2. **The shape is wrong.** Ours is a 32-bar linear equal-power fade (FADE=lin). B sits at -30 to -12 dB for the first 13 bars, then the two decks meet at equal level right where the drums clash.
   A then lingers at -8 dB at bar 232 and -14 dB at bar 236, and is only fully gone at clip 47.6 s.
   Joe (fitted gains): B is in at -2 to -8 dB for 17+ bars and its lows at -4 to -15 dB. A stays at full level, then A and its bass drop 22 dB within 1 bar at clip 24-25.6 s, exactly on B's phrase at bar 32.
3. **The bass handoff leaves a hole.** The SWAP_THIN path (mix_simple.py:99-108) put the swap at mix bars 225.5-229.5. That lands on B's source bar 44, which is a low-end dip (-35 dB).
   Summed lows drop from -21.4 dB at bar 225 to -39.0 dB at bar 228 (clip 31.0 s), about 18 dB. They're still about 9 dB down at bar 229, and B's lows settle 3-6 dB under A's.
   There are no double basslines (the handoff itself works), and the hole sits right under the clash peak.
4. **Structure.** sections.json says Dusk To Dawn's drop1 is bar 56, but the energy shows the drop at bar 64 (bars 62-63 dip, then -17 dB); structure.py agrees, with drop_b=64.
   STRUCT (mix_simple.py:163-164) therefore starts B at 24, and its real drop lands 8 bars after A has gone (mix bar 248, past the end of the clip).

## Why the knobs did nothing (nat_long)
- The window is fixed by the STRUCT bonus, -STRUCTW 300 (mix_simple.py:205): cost 1725 vs 2024 for the next 32-bar window. The recreate.py knobs only re-rank families; they can't move it.
- family_prior_w only applies when prefer_family is set (recreate.py:163-164), and rules.json has prefer_family=null, so it does nothing.
- linger_pen_w and breakdown_pen_w (recreate.py:172-176): window_phase_cost gives linger_c=0 and brk_c=0 for this window. For loop windows, structure.py:225 int(list) raises TypeError, which recreate.py:121-122 swallows and returns 0.
- SWAP_POS: this pair isn't beat-on-beat (bob() is false, mix_simple.py:81-82), so the swap position comes from SWAP_THIN, where POS is only a tie-break (mix_simple.py:104-108). c=18 regardless.
- HIGH_SWAP / HIGH_OFS: high_at() needs bob() (mix_simple.py:88,124), so high_bar is None and there's no high swap.
- The swap_early / swap_late families lose their own SWAP_POS because recreate.py:181 overwrites it with rules env. Their cost also equals nat_long's (the cost ignores env and sneak), so they're never picked.
- What does work: BASS_SWAP_BARS (swap_w=3) and FADE (FADE=tail changed the score).
- Side bugs:
  - The voc proxy is -100 everywhere, so vact is all True (engine.py:24) and V counts every bar (mix_simple.py:133). That gives V=20 and adds +2000 to every 32-bar window, and real vocal overlap goes unchecked.
  - trans['bass_swap'] (mix_simple.py:250) reports POS*X (213.5-216.5), not the real lane (225.5-229.5).

3. Add a real "early-in, phrase-cut" nat_long family: B up on its phrase 16+ bars before the cut, A and its lows out in 1 bar or less on the shared 16-bar boundary. Let family env win over rules env (recreate.py:181), and give family variants costs that actually differ.

5. Wire or flag the dead knobs: prefer_family, a phase-cost exception that logs instead of silently returning 0, and the voc proxy (NOVOC or Demucs). Take drop1 from structure.py, not sections.json.
