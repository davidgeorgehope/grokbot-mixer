# Outro-over-intro bonus, OUTRO_QC, SLOWDOWN, pro-vs-ours (2026-11-19)

All research flags, off by default. `rules.json` and engine defaults unchanged.

## Why the 300-point bonus exists
Added 2026-10-31 (snapshot a6f4ae2, DOCKET "Abrupt-fade fix"). Mix 05 outgoing decks dropped 6 dB at the bass swap, were at -20 dB about 10
bars later, and usually left before their outro. The fix made `STRUCT=1` the default: the outgoing plays its whole outro to its music end and
the incoming is cued so its first drop lands there (modelled on the 5/5 gold blend, Mix05n 55:10 Lilies -> Just Hold On). `STRUCTW=300` is
just "big enough to beat every other engine cost", so that window wins whenever it is feasible. It was never fitted to pro data.

## What pros do (dev, 148 transitions, 19 mixes, 9 DJs; pattern_scan_1119.json; mix-grouped 95% CIs, all keep sign leaving any DJ out)
- Pros' blends reach the outgoing's final 16-bar block 39% of the time; our default 87% (diff -0.48 [-0.66, -0.23]).
- Pros' blends end about 50 bars before the outgoing's music end; ours about 8.
- The incoming's drop lands right at the blend end: pros 12%, ours 80%. Inside the blend: pros 40%, ours 15%.
- Pros start in the outgoing's breakdown 18%, ours 3%. Pros bring the incoming in already in a drop 32%, ours 7%.
- Outgoing slowdowns overall: no clear difference (touch 41% vs 31%). By type, outro / in-drop slowdown starts: pros 2.9%, ours 12.4%.

## OUTRO_QC=1 (primary arm)
Bonus kept, scaled by 1 - deduction. Deduction from pro data (fit_outro_qc_1119.py, halftime/outro_qc.py): L2 logistic of "pro blend reaches
the final outro block" on six yes/no features of that block (slowdown, drums out, snare out, level drop > 6 dB, early fade > 20 dB, thin lows),
deduction = 1 - p(features)/p(clean outro). Dev tracks get leave-their-DJs-out weights. Pro rate of using the final block when the feature is
present vs absent: energy drop 0.33 vs 0.55, thin 0.40 vs 0.55, snare out 0.39 vs 0.53, slowdown 0.45 vs 0.51, fade 0.42 vs 0.50, drums out (n=4).
Leave-DJ-out the deduction does not separate pro-used from pro-skipped blocks (0.127 vs 0.130): the features explain little of why pros leave early.
Ghetto Blaster outro: deduction 0.48 (300 -> 157), not enough to change the window; OUTRO_QC alone renders the same as the default.

## STRUCTB / STRUCT_COND (comparison arms)
`STRUCTB` sets only the bonus (guards keep STRUCTW). STRUCTB=0 on Ghetto Blaster -> exit at 160 over SPY 0-16, cutting drop 2: the bonus is
currently the only thing that keeps the outgoing's last drop playing.
