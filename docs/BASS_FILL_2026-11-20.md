## What was tried

Carry-over of "later bass handoff when the incoming has no bass in the window" (docs/GRAPHCUT_2026-11-13.md, docs/RCA_2026-11-14_bass_cover.md). Already tried and lost: `BASS_COVER` (no swap, lows ride the fader; held-out 34.05 → 32.51), graph-cut fader-follow bass, `DROP_SWAP`, `STRUCT_THIN` / `THIN_DROPCUE`. A later swap inside a 16-bar window is capped by X1 and the gradual-tail rule to about one bar later than the exit-start swap, while the incoming's real bass arrives at bar 14 or later, or not at all, in 26 of the 33 default windows.

`BASS_FILL=1` (off by default, hook in `src2/mix_simple.py` / `src2/engine.py`, logic in `src2/bass_fill.py`) does not move the swap. From one bar before the swap until the incoming's real bass arrives, it replaces the incoming's <150 Hz band with a phrase-aligned 8- or 4-bar loop of that track's first steady drop-bass phrase. Gate: a swap exists; every incoming bar from the swap start to the real-bass bar sits at least 6 dB under the track's own body (the existing thin line); the real bass is within the first X+16 deck bars and at least 2 bars after the swap start; the loop phrase is steady. This is the bass-only, stem-free version of the 'ib' fix the listener picked on 2026-11-02. No threshold was loosened. X1, the swap shape and the crossfader are unchanged.

Job `bf-cohort-1120` (`eval_bass_fill.sh`, variants `bfb1120_base` / `bff1120_fill`) finished 6:26 AM ET, n_fail=0. A 2-pair smoke (`bf-smoke-1120`, `bfs1120_smoke`) had already scored 24.43 and 29.99 on `hybrid_minds_ukf__08` and `joe_syntax_hospital__12`.

## Why the new L1 fails happened

The fill starts one bar before the swap so the handoff has drop bass to land on. Mid-swap, `BASS_LEAD` crosses the low lanes around −10 dB. With a thin incoming intro, the incoming lane is quiet and the sum stays under the louder deck + 3 dB. With the loop in place, both lanes carry body-level bass, and the sum goes 3–5 dB over — right at or past the L1 line, always 2 bars into the 5-bar swap. That is a design clash, not a measurement artifact: filling the hole during the handoff is what L1 is there to stop.

## Prototype (still off by default): start the fill at the swap end

`src2/bass_fill.py` now starts the loop at the swap end (`k0 = ceil(bass_swap[1] − start)`), and requires the real bass at least 2 bars after that. The thin-through-the-handoff gate is unchanged. No threshold was loosened. Tests in `src/tests/test_bass_fill.py` (9 passed).

On the already-rendered fill metas, re-planning with the new start and re-running the official L1 path:

- `netsky_hospital_classics__02`: no fill (its real bass arrives at the swap end). L1 cleared.
- `hybrid_minds_ukf__12` and `__13`: fill kept, now from the swap end (k0 9 and 10). L1 cleared.
- `hybrid_minds_ukf__09`: the default mid-swap L1 (bar 200, over 3.04) comes back, as expected — late fill no longer changes the handoff. 25 of the 26 originally filled pairs would still fill after the swap; only the Netsky pair above would be skipped.

A full 33-pair late-fill rescore was not started. Even if late fill adds no new L1, the early-fill held-out loss (CI excludes 0) came from post-swap bass-curve mismatch with the pro, which late fill still does on 25 pairs. It is not expected to beat the 34.07 / 32.40 floor; it still needs a like-for-like score before anyone listens to an A/B.

## Why it missed, and what is next

Held-out 30.68 vs 34.07 (−3.39, CI excludes 0). The flag does what it says on dips (median 23.6 → 10.1 dB) and does not move placement, but a blanket "loop the incoming's drop bass under a thin intro" fights the pro bass curve and, with the original start, adds mid-handoff L1. the listener's earlier 3/5 on Prime Expansion -> Demons was about this hole, but the scorer — and previous arms that tried to cover it — still prefer leaving it or picking a different section.

Next:

1. Score the late-start fill (`BASS_FILL=1` as it now stands) like-for-like when the box is quiet, including must-pass. Do not promote on dip alone.
2. Keep "incoming thin through the swap" and "post-swap low-end hole" as inputs to the learned ranker, rather than a fixed render loop on every thin intro.
3. If late fill ever holds pro-match or wins on the listener's ear plus hard checks, cut a blind A/B then. Not today.
