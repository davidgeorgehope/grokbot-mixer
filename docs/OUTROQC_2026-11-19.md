# Outro-over-intro bonus, OUTRO_QC, SLOWDOWN, pro-vs-ours (2026-11-19)

All research flags, off by default. `rules.json` and engine defaults unchanged.

## Why the 300-point bonus exists
Added 2026-10-31 (snapshot a6f4ae2, DOCKET "Abrupt-fade fix"). Mix 05 outgoing decks dropped 6 dB at the bass swap, were at -20 dB about 10
bars later, and usually left before their outro. The fix made `STRUCT=1` the default: the outgoing plays its whole outro to its music end and
the incoming is cued so its first drop lands there (modelled on the 5/5 gold blend, Mix05n 55:10 Lilies -> Just Hold On). `STRUCTW=300` is
just "big enough to beat every other engine cost", so that window wins whenever it is feasible. It was never fitted to pro data.

## What pros do (dev, 148 transitions, 19 mixes, 9 DJs; pattern_scan_1119.json; mix-grouped 95% CIs, all keep sign leaving any DJ out)
- Pros' blends reach the outgoing's final 16-bar block 39% of the time; our default 87% (diff -0.48 [-0.66, -0.23]).
- Pros' blends end about 50 bars before the outgoing's music end; ours about 8.
- The incoming's drop lands right at the blend end: pros 12%, ours 80%. Inside the blend: pros 40%, ours 15%.
- Pros start in the outgoing's breakdown 18%, ours 3%. Pros bring the incoming in already in a drop 32%, ours 7%.
- Outgoing slowdowns overall: no clear difference (touch 41% vs 31%). By type, outro / in-drop slowdown starts: pros 2.9%, ours 12.4%.

## OUTRO_QC=1 (primary arm)
Bonus kept, scaled by 1 - deduction. Deduction from pro data (fit_outro_qc_1119.py, halftime/outro_qc.py): L2 logistic of "pro blend reaches
the final outro block" on six yes/no features of that block (slowdown, drums out, snare out, level drop > 6 dB, early fade > 20 dB, thin lows),
deduction = 1 - p(features)/p(clean outro). Dev tracks get leave-their-DJs-out weights. Pro rate of using the final block when the feature is
present vs absent: energy drop 0.33 vs 0.55, thin 0.40 vs 0.55, snare out 0.39 vs 0.53, slowdown 0.45 vs 0.51, fade 0.42 vs 0.50, drums out (n=4).
Leave-DJ-out the deduction does not separate pro-used from pro-skipped blocks (0.127 vs 0.130): the features explain little of why pros leave early.
Ghetto Blaster outro: deduction 0.48 (300 -> 157), not enough to change the window; OUTRO_QC alone renders the same as the default.

## STRUCTB / STRUCT_COND (comparison arms)
`STRUCTB` sets only the bonus (guards keep STRUCTW). STRUCTB=0 on Ghetto Blaster -> exit at 160 over SPY 0-16, cutting drop 2: the bonus is
currently the only thing that keeps the outgoing's last drop playing.

## EXITPOS=1 (structure-based exit / cue score, replaces the rigid bonus)
`halftime/exitpos.py`, fitted by `fit_exitpos_1119.py` (conditional logit: the pro window vs every grid alternative of the same overlap in the same
pair; 148 dev transitions, 9 DJs; per-track leave-their-DJs-out weights). Features: where the outgoing is left (last drop cut by > 16 bars,
around its last drop's end, inside a breakdown, mid-outro; reference = plays to the end) and what the incoming is doing (drop lands inside the
blend, drop comes after the outgoing is gone, enters inside a drop / breakdown; reference = drop lands as the outgoing leaves).
Coefficients (all keep their sign leaving any DJ out): breakdown exit +1.21, incoming drop inside the blend +0.79, drop after -0.26, last
drop tail -0.71, mid-outro -1.04, cut last drop -1.81 (per window; there are many cut windows), incoming enters in a drop -1.12 / breakdown -1.62.
Scale 110 per logit unit (spread = STRUCTW). Guards kept hard (+600): blend may not start before the last 8 bars of the outgoing's last drop;
the incoming may not enter after its first drop. SLOWDOWN still applies.
Held-out (leave-DJ-out) fit quality is weak: log-lik -5.68 vs -5.92 uniform. Only 18% of pro windows satisfy both guards (107/148 start before
the outgoing's last 8 bars of last drop; median pro leaves 59 bars of the outgoing unplayed). Within the guards the rigid rule is already close
to the pros (exit error 2 bars vs the model's 5; cue error 9 vs 9). So under "don't cut tracks short", EXITPOS mostly changes the cue
(incoming drop inside the blend, longer blends), not the exit. Ghetto Blaster: EXITPOS alone -> 32-bar 192-224 over SPY 48-80 (drop inside);
+ SLOWDOWN -> 192-208 over 48-64 (the posted fix).

### EXITPOS v1 guard bug (fixed 12:45 ET)
v1's payoff guard (blend start >= last drop end - 8) was infeasible on tracks that end right after their last drop and for any 32-bar blend,
so it fired on every window and stopped guarding; with the bonus removed, v1 cut into the outgoing's last drop on 33 of 105 dev pairs
(dev renders ep1119 / eps1119, kept for the record, not used). v2: the outgoing must play its last drop to its end (or to the latest exit
the pair allows), the blend may overlap at most the last 32 bars of that drop, and the latest allowed exit counts as "plays to the end".
Rerun as job ep2-1119 (variants ep2_1119 / ep2s_1119, cohort cs_ep2_1119 / cs_ep2s_1119, report ep2_eval_1119.json).

### Results so far
Held-out (cohort, test / train vs floor 34.07 / 32.40): QC 34.07 / 32.06, SLOW 34.07 / 33.19, QC+SLOW 34.07 / 32.84, STRUCTB=100 31.70 / 32.67,
STRUCTB=0 28.36 / 27.50. Only 1 of 12 test pairs (wilkinson__33) has its default blend start inside an outgoing outro / in-drop slowdown and
the rule does not move it, so the flat test score says nothing about SLOWDOWN; on train it fires on Ghetto Blaster (+16.5) and moves nothing else.
Dev, changed pairs only (hard fail, mix-grouped 95% CI): QC 3 -> 1 of 7 (-0.29 [-0.67, 0.00]); SLOW 6 -> 4 of 12 (-0.17 [-0.56, 0.14]);
QC+SLOW 8 -> 5 of 18 (-0.17 [-0.44, 0.06]). Outgoing bars left unplayed rise (SLOW median 1 -> 16.5) because the slowdown outro is skipped.

## Follow-up: EXITPOS_GATE

EXITPOS v2 lost on held-out (EP 31.94, EPS 30.87 vs 34.07). The gate that keeps outro-over-intro as the default and only exits earlier when it is safe is in `docs/EXITPOS_GATE_2026-11-19.md` (flag `EXITPOS_GATE=1`, off by default, `rules.json` unchanged).
