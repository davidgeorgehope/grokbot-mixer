## What it does

`src2/window_planner.py` nominates windows from both decks' section
analysis, alongside the default outro-over-intro:

- `drop1_end`: outgoing window ends at the first-drop / breakdown boundary,
  incoming starts in a drop (the 11-14 fix: Prime Expansion 96-112 over
  Demons 48-64).
- `breakdown_drop`: outgoing plays its breakdown, incoming starts in a drop
  (the 11-15 pro move). Incoming drop starts may sit past the usual bar-80
  cap when enough of the track is left.

A selector then chooses per pair from track features, with a matching fader
(SNAP_IN's late rise + shallow outgoing hold for breakdown into drop;
default fade for outro-intro and for drop1_end). It is not a blanket swap.

## The locked rule (train-only)

Stay on the default outro-intro window unless all of these hold:

1. The default window has an engine bass hole for more than half the blend
   (`hole_min(X) = X/2`, so 8 bars of a 16-bar window). The dB line is
   still `GAP_DB`. A 5-bar hole (the joe_syntax_hospital__11 shape that
   collapsed under a looser hint-family gate) is left alone.
2. A canonical mid-track window has strictly fewer hole bars, and its
   incoming side is actually in a drop with bass within `GAP_DB` of its body.
3. Either the best outro-intro nomination is also mostly a hole, or the
   incoming intro is short (under 40 bars, shorter than the usual 48).

Then: a short intro prefers `breakdown_drop` + SNAP_IN; otherwise
`drop1_end` with the default fade. On the 21 train pairs this was the
best of the small grid that also hit both listening targets and left
joe_syntax_hospital__11 on its default (exact type 10/21, mid-vs-intro
14/21, no false mid-track swaps on train pairs whose pro used an intro
cue). Seven missed mid-track pros mostly already have a clean default
(0 hole bars), which the rule correctly leaves alone.

Held-out similarity scores were not used to pick this rule. The flag
stays off unless a like-for-like cohort does not drop held-out 34.05
and introduces 0 fails.

## Hook

- `mix_simple.pair_cands` appends the nominated mid-track windows when
  `WINDOW_PLANNER` is on, so they are legal `out_win` values (including
  incoming starts past bar 80).
- `recreate.recreate` adds `plan_breakdown_drop` / `plan_drop1_end`
  families and, when the selector picks a mid-track window, uses that
  pick and its fade env. Off, the engine is unchanged.

Tests: `src/tests/test_window_planner.py`.

## BASS_COVER fallback (thin-intro stays only)

A second pass on the 11-14 window (`docs/RCA_2026-11-14_bass_cover.md`)
found that the official bass-gap test is blind to holes wider than its
+-4-bar neighbourhood, `swap_before_thin` barely sees a fully thin
window, and FADE=long bypasses `SWAP_THIN`. `BASS_COVER` (keep lows on
the outgoing when every incoming overlap bar is >= 6 dB under its body)
cut that pair's dip from 22.5 to 18.3 dB, but a global on-switch scored
held-out 32.51 (train 33.18).

The planner therefore uses it only as a fallback: when it *keeps* an
outro-over-intro window, the incoming overlap is fully thin, and the
default already has a majority engine hole. Mid-track picks do not get
it (the 11-14 / 11-15 targets switch away from the thin intro). The
6 dB thin line and `GAP_DB` are unchanged. The bass-gap neighbourhood
blind spot itself is a later pro-p95 calibration, not a loosened test.

## What was still missing on ukf__25 (listener: 3.5/5)

the listener, 11:01 AM ET on 15 November 2026, after listening to the planner
clips: Prime Expansion -> Demons (drop1_end) was "really nice"; Cinnamon
-> Together (breakdown_drop + SNAP_IN at Together bar 64) was "closer

(NNLS deck gains, 174 BPM bars).

Ranked gaps vs the pro:

1. **Incoming cue (largest).** The pro cues Together at 263.6 s, bar
   191, in the third drop's body. The first planner pick used bar 64,
   the start of drop 2 — 127 bars early, a different drop. Cinnamon was
   already close: breakdown into the start of drop 2 (bar ~122-130 at
   entry; our window ended at 128). A fader-only change cannot fix this.
2. **Outgoing hold length.** After the entry the pro stays audible for
   26-27 bars at about -10 to -15 dB (mean first 8: all -12.9, bass
   -13.6, mids -10.5, highs -5.6). Default SNAP_IN only holds through
   the 16-bar window plus a ~4-bar residual, so the outgoing is gone
   far too soon. The 44 s daily B clip itself is too short to hear the
   full 26-bar tail; the 64 s pro.wav is the right reference.
3. **EQ split during the hold.** After the swap, outgoing highs linger
   about 8 dB above the bass. SNAP_IN is a wideband fader, so it cannot
   keep the tops up while the lows sit lower. Not prototyped in this
   pass.
4. **Incoming rise.** The pro slams about 19.6 dB in one bar (4.9
   dB/beat), over the X1 3.0 cap. The legal two-bar rise at 2.95
   dB/beat is already what SNAP_IN does. Not loosened.
5. **Phrase.** Bar 191 is 1 bar from the 32-bar phrase at 192. Once the
   last-drop body cue is used, this is a minor grid offset, not a
   separate window problem.
