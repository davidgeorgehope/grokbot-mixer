## What was tried
- the listener's note: bringing a mix in over a hard slowdown sounds weird. The earlier `SLOWDOWN=1` flag only penalises a blend that *starts* inside an outgoing outro / in-drop slowdown, treats every 8-bar snare dip the same, and never looks at the incoming track. It was already scored on 2026-11-19 (held-out flat at 34.07; train 33.19 because Ghetto Blaster moved).
- Shorter-tail was not retried: `TAIL_EXIT=6` is already the default (docs/TAILEXIT_2026-11-15.md).
- `SLOWPAIR=1` (off by default, hook in `protrans/recreate.py`, logic in `src2/slowpair.py`) is pair-aware:
  - Hard spans (pre-declared): half-time sections from `halftime_tg.py`; `slowdown.py` runs of at least 16 bars that are outro, in-drop or breakdown; intro-only runs of at least 24 bars.
  - Case A: the blend starts inside an outgoing hard outro / in-drop, or at least 12 bars of the window sit in one. Starting a few bars before the slowdown (the Ghetto Blaster fix) is allowed. Breakdowns stay allowed.
  - Case B: the incoming cue sits on a hard span for at least 8 bars, **and** the outgoing is still in a drop. Outro-over-intro stays the safe default once the outgoing has left its drop.
- Detector input: `ht-scan-1120` (job done 12:03 AM ET). Alternative windows come from already-scored engine candidates, with the last-drop payoff guard when the exit moves earlier.

## Why it missed, and what is next

Held-out 32.11 vs 34.07 (-1.96). 11 picks moved (6 gained, 5 lost on the similarity score); 0 clashing defaults had no legal alternative. Worst held-out move: `maduk_liquicity_lunar2025__03` 39.32 → 21.92 (Sub Focus & Wilkinson - Freedom (High Contrast Remix) -> LSB - New Day). Case B cue moves (skip a hard incoming intro while the outgoing is still in a drop) are the likely drag: they fight the outro-over-intro default on pairs where the scorer still likes the later bars of a long intro. Case A exit moves also lost on at least one pair.

Next: keep the hard-span features (outgoing outro/in-drop cover, incoming hard intro under a still-hot outgoing drop, half-time flags) as inputs to the model picker, instead of a hand cost that always avoids them. Case A alone is the closer reading of the listener's note and only fires on a few pairs; a Case-A-only arm is the obvious follow-up if the combined flag lost because of Case B cue moves.
