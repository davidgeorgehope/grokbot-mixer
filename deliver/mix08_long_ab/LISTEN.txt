Mix 08 A/B clips. Audio only.

Each file is one real blend from the mix. The filename has the two track names and the time inside the clip where the blend starts.
Every clip starts 16 bars before that blend and ends 8 bars after the outgoing track is gone (A and B of the same number share the same window, long enough for both versions).
A short tick marks the exact blend start (always 16 bars / about 0:22 into the file, unless the mix began later).
A and B of the same number are the same blend and the same window, two versions, labelled blindly.

The times when the outgoing track is fully gone are in the accompanying message (they can differ between A and B).
