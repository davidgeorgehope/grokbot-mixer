## Ranked causes

### 1. The incoming intro sits 151 ms early of its own grid, and the engine never beatmatches the two decks (root cause)

`prep.grid()` / `halfbeat.fix_grid()` fit one phase for the whole track and only test a half-beat flip. Ghetto Blaster's drop 2 is on that grid; its intro is 0.438 beat (151 ms) early, with template strength 5.5–5.7. We Are One's outro is 65 ms early of *its* grid. `engine._bars` then places every bar at `d0 + bar * BAR` on a perfect 174 grid. During the short loud overlap the decks are ~80–130 ms apart (loud-bar xcorr **+123.4 ms**, template dphase 86.2 ms, pro-lock 109.4 ms). That is exactly the "beat clashes" the listener heard.

A 80–130 ms (quarter-to-third beat) error is a dead zone for the existing gates: halfbeat.py only compares whole vs half-beat, and the render-time gate needs both `phase_fail` and `lag_fail` (`rh ≥ 0.3`). This pair is `phase_fail=true`, `lag_fail=false`, so `fail=false` and nothing moves.

### 2. Phrase-to-grid (FINE_PHASE) is the wrong model, and does not fully lock

An earlier attempt nudged each 8-bar phrase onto the mix grid (`FINE_PHASE=1`). That cut pro-lock from 109.4 ms to 46.5 ms and raised identity only from 1.26 to 15.56, but the official score fell **58.04 → 38.46**. Two reasons:

- The template dphase (86.2 ms) is not the same number as the loud-bar xcorr lag (+123.4 ms). After the phrase nudges, about 37–46 ms of relative lag is still on the clash — still audible, still over the 15 ms F1 line.
- FINE_PHASE also nudged the *outgoing* outro by +64.7 ms, which moved the whole mix relative to the pro. Stem curves 75.83 → 50.1, deck curves 59.67 → 30.29, bass swap 51.12 → 24.86, outgoing cut 65.65 → 40.98, align lag 2020 → 2194 ms. Identity improved a little; everything else got worse.

A DJ does not re-grid the record that is already playing. They slip the incoming record until the kicks lock. That is `BLEND_LOCK`: measure the signed kick+snare lag on bars where both decks are loud (within 6 dB and above −40 dB, longest consecutive run), and shift *only* the incoming deck by that lag, in the same sign as `engine.nudge` (positive = events later in the mix). On this pair that lag is **+123.4 ms**. A lock already under 15 ms, or one past 150 ms (near half a beat), is left alone; half-beat cases stay with halfbeat.py. X1, GAP_DB, F1/N1 thresholds are unchanged.

### 3. Why the scorer then reports `beat_off`, and why 46 ms was not "close enough"

`score.beat_off_ms` compares the aligned *ours mix* to the *pro mix*, and only searches ±40 ms. The 109 ms deck-vs-deck flam was outside that window, so the clip the listener heard was scored `beat_off_ms = 0.0` even though the decks were a third of a beat apart. After a real lock the residual falls inside the window and the term becomes visible (8.71 ms on the official after render). That is not a new failure; it is the scorer finally seeing a small leftover. `beat_identity = 100 * exp(−|ours_lock − pro_lock| / 25)` was the term that actually caught this pair (1.26 before).

A 46 ms leftover (FINE_PHASE) is still well over the 15 ms F1 line and still audible. A real alignment fix has to get pro-lock under 15 ms.

### 4. Why `BLEND_LOCK` did not engage by default on this `nat_short` n/240 window

Two bugs, not a window-family carve-out:

1. **The flag defaulted off.** It was treated like a style / pending-eval switch. Beatmatching two overlapping decks is a correctness fix, so once the cohort holds it should be on whenever the decks overlap at an audible level (both within 6 dB and above −40 dB, longest consecutive run) and the lag is a *confident* 15–150 ms cluster. The window family does not matter; this pair is a normal `nat_short` 16-bar overlap at bars 224–240.
2. **The official path never ran the hook.** `blend_bench.render` (recreate and the 33-pair eval) always plans with `mix_simple --dry` and then calls `engine.render_deck` itself. The first hook ran `lock_decks` only on the full-mix path, *after* the `--dry` `sys.exit()`. Dry meta had no `blend_lock_ms`, the official after clip was byte-identical to the before clip, and the score stayed 58.04 with loud lag still 123.4 ms. The hook now runs *before* the dry exit. A regression test (`test_dry_plan_writes_blend_lock_ms`) covers that path and has to use the official fade rules (`FADE=long`, `TAIL_EXIT=6`, …): without them the equally-loud region slides to bars 230–234 and the estimator reports a bogus −108 ms.

### 5. The leftover "drift" at bars 229–230 was a 2-bar xcorr wrap, not residual audio lag

A B-vs-B check shows the incoming audio was delayed a uniform 123.4 ms on every bar. After that shift:

- Bar 228 is 0.0 ms on 1-bar and 2-bar windows.
- Bar 230 is **−1.3 ms on a 1-bar window** (kicks and snares in that bar only). The 2-bar window includes fading bar 231, so the same xcorr hops to a 149 ms wrap.
- Bar 229's 2-bar window has a 164 ms edge peak, but a 0.0 ms secondary at relative strength 0.80.

The first after-PNG plotted the 2-bar wraps and looked like leftover drift. The lock measurement now uses 1-bar windows (so bar 230 contributes +121.9 ms to the pre-lock cluster instead of −50.8). The diagnostic takes a 1-bar value, or a 2-bar interior / near-zero peak when the 1-bar argmax has wrapped. On the current after PNG, bars 227–230 sit in the ±15 ms band (median 1.5 ms). Pro-lock is 14.7 ms. the listener heard no clash.

### 6. A loose estimator must not become the default: it unlocked already-aligned pairs

A first 33-pair run with a 2-bar / 50 ms / 2-member cluster (variant `rca1117_blendlock`, stopped after 13 pairs) applied false lags to blends that were already locked, e.g. `netsky_hospital_classics__02` offs `[152, −61]` → applied −61 ms, pro-lock 2.4 → 57.6, identity 90.8 → 10.0, score 43.75 → 37.89 (−5.86). Similar hits: `__17` −6.70, `wilkinson_boomtown2026__33` −4.27, `hybrid_minds_ukf__08` −3.98, `joe_syntax_hospital__01` −4.34. This pair itself was the genuine gain (58.04 → 60.51, lock 109.4 → 14.7).

The apply gate is now: 1-bar windows, 20 ms cluster radius, **at least 3 agreeing bars**, the cluster a **majority** of the loud run, cluster span ≤ 25 ms, every member at least 15 ms from zero (so a 10 ms already-locked bar cannot be swallowed into a 17 ms "lock"), and still only if 15 ≤ |lag| ≤ 150. This pair's 1-bar offs `[130.6, 123.4, 30.5, 121.9]` still apply +123.4 (3-of-4, span 8.7 ms). The misfire shapes above now return "inconsistent / too few agreeing bars" and leave the incoming deck alone. Covered by `test_confident_lag_accepts_this_pair_and_rejects_misfires`.
